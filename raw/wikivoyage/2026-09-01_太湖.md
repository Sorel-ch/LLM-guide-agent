'''太湖'''是[[中国]]一个广大而且风景秀美的湖泊，位于[[江苏]]和[[浙江]]的交界处。太湖风景区以其美丽的风景吸引来很多周边大城市——[[上海]]、[[杭州]]和[[南京]]——以及周边城市的人前来旅游。某些程度上也是[[华东]]地区乡村旅行的主要去处。

这里是长江三角洲地区的平原，湖泊附近没有任何高山但有一些小山丘，湖水的平均深度只有2米。太湖面积2,250平方千米，在中国五大淡水湖中位列第三。从很小的岛到几平方千米的岛，湖中大大小小共有约90座岛屿。

==城市==
[[File:Yuantouzhu,Lake Tai.JPG|thumb|无锡附近的宝塔]]
太湖周边的主要城市：
* [[无锡]]在太湖北侧
* [[苏州]]在东北方
* [[吴江]]在东侧
* [[湖州]]在南侧
* [[宜兴]]在西侧

这些城市中，无锡和吴江紧邻太湖而其他的则更靠近内陆。有1000万人口的苏州是附近最大的城市；无锡大约有650万人，湖州和宜兴则都是250万人口。官方上，吴江在2012年成为苏州市的一个市辖区，人口150万。

所有这些城市都已存在了几十个世纪而且有具有一定的历史意义的和有趣的老建筑。特别是苏州，他在历史上就是一个重要的城市而且也是许多中国游客爱去的目的地。

==其他目的地==

太湖是中国著名运河，[[京杭大运河沿线|京杭大运河]]的一部分，这是一个从杭州到[[北京]]的途经1800千米的伟大工程奇迹。

==了解==

[[File:Taihu Lake Suzhou.jpg|thumb|苏州附近的太湖]]
太湖生产鱼和螃蟹，周围的地区是平坦和肥沃。<!-- The region has been inhabited since prehistoric times, always fairly densely by the standards of the era. It has been a major producer of silk and tea for centuries, and has recently become quite highly developed in terms of modern industry; in particular, Suzhou and Wuxi are major centers of China's booming hi-tech sector. Today the main cities around the lake have a combined population of twenty-odd million, roughly that of [[Shanghai]] or [[Australia]]; counting smaller towns and the countryside, the total population is considerably higher.

In the third millennium BCE &mdash; while cities such as [[Thebes]], [[Nineveh]] and [[Mohenjo-daro]] flourished further West &mdash; the lake area was the center of the Liangzhu Culture, now known mainly by the high-quality jade artifacts they left behind. In some ways they were quite advanced for the time period, with extensive irrigation and some cities, but they were still a Neolithic (late Stone Age) culture; they were to be the last such culture in this region. The people involved are thought to have been of the Austronesian ethnic/linguistic group, more closely related to today's Filipinos, Malays, Indonesians and Polynesians than to modern Chinese. Liangzhu is an area in the suburbs of [[Hangzhou]] with a major [http://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5805/ archaeological site] where one of their cities is being excavated. There is a [http://www.lzmuseum.cn/ museum] nearby.

The Zhou Dynasty (1046-256 BCE) conquered the lake region and the cultures of the Yangtze and Yellow River basins became integrated, forming the core of modern Chinese civilisation. Of course there were still regional differences. The lake region was the center of the Wu culture which first developed in the late Zhou period. In the Three Kingdoms period (220-280 CE) the Wu Kingdom was one of the three, both independent and important; its capital was [[Suzhou]]. The language of the area is still called [[Wu phrasebook|Wu]]. 

Today the whole lake region, especially around Suzhou and Wuxi, has well-developed tourist facilities. There are good roads, many parks, some beaches, hotels from large luxury resorts to fairly small and mundane, and restaurants over a similar range.

Industrialisation has created some fairly severe pollution in the lake. The government claim to have cleaned up most of it and to be hard at work on the rest. Critics say very little has actually been done.-->

==语言==

[[吴语]]是周边的传统语言，现在仍在使用。如今通常被称为“上海方言”或“上海话”，但苏州口音仍然最负盛名。

==购物==

太湖周边区域生产中国最好的丝绸。[[杭州]]与[[苏州]]皆因丝绸而闻名，许多制成品包括令人惊叹的刺绣作品。[[南京]]也有精美的刺绣。[[湖州]]是一个主要的桑蚕养殖区，为杭州提供了丝绸产业的原材料的中心。

该地区也产生了许多陶器。特别是，[[宜兴]]产生极多的精炻器壶与传统特产。

湖州附近的[[安吉]]因竹子而闻名，还有竹子博物馆，这里也生产许多手工竹制品。

在旅行区，你经常需要[[讨价还价]]来得到一个合理的价位。

==饮食==

太湖生产的鱼和螃蟹，都是当地常见的美食。

该地区产生许多精美的茶，主要是绿茶。中国最有名的绿茶，龙井茶来自附近的杭州，这些远销世界各地，尤其在[[日本]]流行。

==安全==
该地区没有特别需要注意的危险，但是中国的任何地方（或者实际上在全[[亚洲]]），瓶装水要比自来水更安全。同时旅行者要注意旅行一般注意点和预防[[小偷]]和[[常见的诈骗|旅游诈骗]]。
